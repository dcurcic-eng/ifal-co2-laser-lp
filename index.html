Still wonderingabout anything?

Precision CO2 resurfacing combined with fractional technology, targeted results with less downtime than traditional CO2 lasers.

Most patients see dramatic improvement after a single deep session. Lighter, micro-ablative settings may be planned as a short series of two to three. Your physician confirms the right depth and count at your consultation.

We apply medical-grade topical numbing before treatment to keep you as comfortable as possible. Most patients describe the sensation as warmth with some prickling. You can drive yourself home the same day.

Fresh, smoother skin appears as soon as peeling completes, around days 7-10. Collagen keeps rebuilding for 3-6 months, so firmness and texture continue to improve long after the redness fades.
